# Underwriting and forecast evaluation

## What the analysis measures

Business growth is different from per-share growth. The models separate operating performance, cash conversion, net share retirement and the valuation paid at the horizon. They do not add a buyback yield to a target price that already includes the smaller share count. Announced repurchase authorization is not treated as cash spent. Reported repurchase spending is checked against actual net share changes and future cash available.

Trailing figures use the latest annual result plus current comparable interim result minus prior comparable interim result. Interim cash is not simply doubled or multiplied by four when seasonality or customer-deposit timing matters. Current shares are an explicit dated denominator, distinct from weighted-average diluted shares in reported EPS. Use USD millions consistently before calculating per-share values.

Reported free cash flow starts with GAAP operating cash less cash capital expenditure. Company adjustments are shown separately. Customer balances, merchant deposits, loan-sale timing, equipment-lease principal, restructuring and tariff refunds receive company-specific treatment. A deduction for reported stock compensation is a conservative economic proxy; it is not an actual cash outflow or an exact measurement of replacement purchases. Keep that sensitivity visible.

The forward price model uses one named valuation measure per company, with a separate cash/GAAP reasonableness check. Do not add two valuations together. When net share retirement is projected, constrain repurchase funding against cash generated after required spending and compensation replacement. Avoid treating debt-funded repurchases as recurring operating improvement. The terminal multiple and future drivers are author judgments, not observed market consensus.

## Scenario discipline

Each selected pitch gets bear/base/bull operating inputs, valuation sensitivity, a dated central price forecast and measurable disconfirmation conditions. Scenarios are not probability-weighted unless an empirical, inspectable calibration supports probabilities. A central estimate is a forecast, not a guarantee or a statistically estimated expected value. Positive recommendations require plausible value creation at the frozen entry price; a good company at an unsupported price can be rejected in the candidate screen.

Record correlated exposures across selections. Four ideas are not a portfolio allocation recommendation. No user holdings, risk tolerance or capital amount is presumed. High-stakes accuracy calls for primary current sources; uncertainty belongs next to the specific assumption it changes.

## Frozen benchmark contract

Use the October 12, 2026 regular-session closes in `prices-20261012.json`. Evaluate on the frozen October 12, 2028 US trading session (a Thursday), using the same session for every stock and SPY. An unexpected exchange closure must be documented before scoring with a separately versioned calendar exception; the evaluator rejects silent date changes. Reconcile splits, spin-offs, cash acquisitions and other corporate actions before comparing prices. Do not drop a delisted or failed company from the sample.

Primary comparison: split-adjusted stock price return versus split-adjusted SPY price return. Secondary comparison, when complete distributions can be verified: shareholder cash return including cash dividends, with the same non-reinvestment convention for SPY. Dividend estimates in scenarios are labeled forecasts; realized distributions determine actual results. Entry quotes do not claim a historical total-return adjustment that the connected tool did not expose.

Report all four outcomes, the stock's absolute price error relative to the frozen entry price, forecast-direction success, and excess price return over SPY. Include the median/mean error and compare against a simple flat-price forecast. Preserve the original registry and Git identity when updating the thesis later; a revised forecast is a new dated observation, not a replacement for the original. These measures establish a transparent evaluation method, not current prediction accuracy. No ongoing scheduler is created by this task.

## Sources and writing

Source registers bind actual facts to exact primary URLs, publication dates, fiscal periods, units and statement sections. Reported facts, management guidance and author projections remain distinct in tables and figure labels. Current-data claims after the cutoff are excluded. SEC and IR documents sometimes duplicate the same disclosure; duplicate hosting does not create independent corroboration.

The manuscripts lead with the specific mispricing thesis, then explain the operational mechanism, current evidence, reverse valuation, forecast, catalysts and the strongest contrary case. Tables and original figures communicate calculations that change the investment conclusion. Operational build notes stay here, outside the public reader.
