# Texas business research

The publication's promise: explain a Texas development well enough that an operator, founder, finance leader, or investor can make a better decision. Authority should come from inspectable work, a coherent body of research, and useful follow-through. Public biography and credentials remain factual.

## Research library

- Power availability: connection evidence, SB6 implementation, capital at risk, and interruption design.
- AI regulation: private, governmental, healthcare, and privacy responsibilities translated into procurement decisions.
- Commercial contracts: HB40's separate monetary tests and September 2026 decisions on counterclaims and signed promises.
- Water: legal supply, peak delivery, recoverable storage, drought and design dependencies.
- Property tax: the temporary appraisal limitation, personal-property exemption and cost after a deal.
- Data-center sales tax: certification routes, investment clocks, purchaser eligibility and repayment risk.
- JETI: the school M&O value benefit, competing locations and continuing operating obligations.
- Franchise tax: threshold, information reports, computation choice and the current research-credit application.
- Hiring: correctly dated metro and occupational measures connected to recruiting and ramp costs.
- Cross-border logistics: matching denominators, customs, commercial schedules and full-route economics.
- Manufacturing: company-reported stages translated into buyer-specific qualification and allocation questions.
- Commercial insurance: perils, settlement, dollar retention and the business-income recovery case.
- Robotaxi comparison: current passenger access, onboard roles, booking workflows and paid exceptions.
- Waymo travel: Texas admission channels, the airport leg, business payment and employee time.
- Zoox market entry: local fleet stages, conditional federal permission, depot dependencies and utilization.
- Trucking operators: selected industrial, regional and highway programs, human roles and usable capacity.
- Freight route economics: direct service versus transfers, accepted loads, setup, recovery and backup.
- AV authorization: holder and vehicle records, responder plans, continuing duties and service continuity.

## Commissioning standard

Every brief begins with a reader and a decision: commit capital, choose a site, price a contract, hire, procure technology, manage compliance, acquire a company, or change an operating plan. A news event qualifies when it changes that decision. Use an evergreen explanation for the underlying mechanism and a dated update for a material development; revise the established URL when the core question remains the same.

Research the current enacted text, agency order, docket, primary financial disclosure, or official dataset. Check publication date, observation period, geographic scope, units, denominators, implementation status, and subsequent changes. Separate legislation from rulemaking, an announcement from completion, and a forecast from observed results. Secondary reporting can help find an issue; it cannot substitute for the controlling source. Do not manufacture interviews, field experience, demand metrics, or legal credentials.

The finished article needs a direct opening, primary links beside material claims, original business analysis, a worked comparison or declared hypothetical, a usable checklist, and explicit update triggers. Preserve useful qualifications beside the claim. A source ledger identifies what each source supports. Keep private production notes outside the public copy. The initial-edition receipt protects a new manuscript; historical restoration/revision receipts remain intact.

## Discovery standard

Use one canonical document per distinct decision question; write a precise search title and readable editorial headline; keep visible prose, static HTML, metadata, and machine projections aligned. Provide a concise answer, descriptive section targets, relevant questions with source links, a coherent reading path, and links to related investigations. Generate sitemap/feed entries and Article/Person/CollectionPage references from the published catalog. Keep dates tied to actual content changes. Do not promise indexing, a ranking, a rich result, or a citation by an AI service.

Google's current [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) supports useful original content and a clear crawlable structure. The site's existing optional machine-readable formats remain a discovery aid; they are not evidence of ranking or retrieval. No paid-link purchases, outreach, or new crawling permissions are part of this goal.

## Maintenance and measurement

Recheck a story when its controlling rule becomes effective, an agency issues an order, a court decision is reversed or clarified, a financing or ownership event closes, or an underlying dataset receives a new observation. Record the actual new evidence and update the same article when its decision question remains stable.

After an authorized release, establish a dated page/query baseline in Search Console and a landing-page baseline in analytics. Compare equivalent reporting windows, keeping publication lag and brand/nonbrand queries explicit. Look for relevant query discovery, engaged reading, returning visitors, source downloads, and attributable business inquiries. Treat AI referrals and independently observed citations as separate evidence. Do not present article counts or schema validation as proof of authority growth.

The runnable manual workflow is in [Publishing and updates](PUBLISHING.md), with readers, decisions and review triggers in [library.json](library.json). No recurring automation or external outreach has been created. A future cadence or monitoring request requires its own defined scope. Task continuation evidence stays in the local source-owned work record.
