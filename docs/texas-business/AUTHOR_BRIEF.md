# The Texas library: what to know and explain

Read the eighteen finished investigations through their decision examples. The core skill is to ask which document, date, and operating boundary supports a business commitment. Use the questions below to practice explaining the finding from its evidence, rather than memorize a headline.

## Power before property

Statewide requested demand is different from a site's usable allocation. Price, connection studies, conditional classification, energization, and commissioning establish different things. The October 8 standards affect security and construction exposure; the separate audit and permitting processes affect timing. The example shows why allocated capacity, cash support, sunk construction, and delay loss need different accounts.

Be able to explain why two sites with the same allocated MW can have different financeable economics, why security is not the electricity bill, and why an audit-report date is not an opening date. Scope this explanation to ERCOT; do not present statewide queue totals as built demand or name a specific site's date without its documents.

## Texas AI law before deployment

Start with the operator and use. TRAIGA's public-sector provisions, healthcare notice, private-business prohibitions, and privacy/biometric responsibilities overlap differently. The support-to-financing example shows why a changed purpose deserves a new review even when the software subscription stays the same. A vendor's general compliance claim is weaker than an inspectable use, data, contract, and oversight record.

Be able to explain why the TDPSA small-business threshold does not automatically exempt a company from TRAIGA, why intent language in one prohibition does not settle other discrimination laws, and why a NIST policy alone is not the specified liability protection. Refer case-specific legal interpretation to counsel; business-sandbox intake and comprehensive federal preemption remain unverified.

## The bargain, the claim, the forum

Transaction consideration, qualifying claims, forum authority, and preserved promises are separate questions. The Bakery illustrates a change caused by pleaded counterclaims; Beard illustrates the importance of actual reliance language and contractual context. The hypothetical $12M agreement shows why contract face value is not the amount-in-controversy calculation.

Be able to explain why a threat differs from a pleading, why Beard is not a rule that every merger clause defeats fraud, and why eight historical trials cannot establish a speed advantage. Preserve the court report's April cutoff and the opinions' trial-court status.

## Water, property and recovery

Water: annual allocation, peak-day delivery, instantaneous flow, usable storage and recovery time are separate measures. Be able to explain why the two-site example meets annual demand at both locations but exposes a daily constraint at one. Trace source authority through supplier commitments, drought terms, treatment, reuse and discharge. Know why an adopted plan's recommended project is not a delivery agreement and why the Phase II amendment's December consideration remains tentative at this cutoff.

Property tax: value, rate and contractual cost allocation are separate inputs. Be able to reproduce the $72,000/$90,000 sensitivity, explain the 2026 circuit-breaker expiry, and distinguish the $125,000 personal-property value exemption from a cash tax credit. Use the applicable ownership, location and aggregation rule; a tenant's reimbursement and conditional protest role need their own evidence.

Insurance: explain a specific event through peril, property, settlement, deductible and suspended income. Be able to calculate a percentage deductible using its stated denominator and describe why the cheaper hypothetical premium retains $260,000 more of the same loss. TWIA's filing is not an individual quote. NFIP building/contents limits and deductibles are separate; its program does not supply business-interruption recovery. TWIA-17 is a linked form example, with its actual trigger and time-deductible qualifications.

## Tax benefits and obligations

Data-center tax: distinguish the standard state-only eligible-purchase exemption from the large state-and-local route. Explain why eligible investment is not the exempt-purchase base, why the large project's earlier application/certification investment clock differs from exemption start, and why an agreement to contract for 20MW is not an executed power allocation. Be able to calculate the $10M saving and $3.2M local tax under the declared standard-route example, then describe the repayment scenario without inventing a failure probability.

JETI: locate the benefit in the school M&O line and reproduce the $5.85M/$4.95M full-bill comparison. Explain competing-site evidence, eligible-property timing, 1600-hour qualifying jobs, wages, bond and the compliance tail. The J0013 record teaches why posting, signing, incentive years and actual operating results belong in different fields.

Franchise: explain the information report separately from zero tax, annualize a short period for the threshold screen, and reproduce the $28,125/$29,790 method comparison using its declared assumptions. Know why an ordinary filing extension and the separate refundable-research-credit application are different tasks. Keep eligibility, report year, old exemption use, required forms and actual deadline with the preparer's workpaper.

## Workforce, trade and usable capacity

Hiring: distinguish adjusted/unadjusted data, establishment jobs/people, mean/median pay, reference period/publication date and wage/full employer cost. Explain why 20.8 days is a break-even sensitivity, not evidence that a $2 increase causes faster hiring. Use real qualified-candidate, offer, start and training cohorts before changing the plan.

Logistics: explain why two-direction all-mode freight dollars divided by one-direction truck entries is an invalid ratio. Name the actual bridge, direction, commercial/inspection/broker/receiving windows and origin basis. Reproduce the $410K/$406K example and show how one additional incident changes the route preference. A supplier address in Mexico does not determine USMCA eligibility or all applicable tariffs.

Manufacturing: distinguish planned investment, a shell, pilot completion, production, product qualification, allocated output and accepted delivery. Know what TI, Samsung and GlobalWafers actually report at the October 12 cutoff. Reproduce the 40,375/39,950 component sensitivity without treating its inputs as company yields. Material wafers, finished devices, process nodes and wafer diameters are different measures and products.

## Robotaxis, fleet stages and freight economics

Passenger access: explain why an open city, an admitted rider, a qualifying request, an optional AV match and a reserved vehicle are different facts. Know the reviewed Waymo channels, Zoox waitlist/testing stages, Tesla rider rules and Avride's current supervised Dallas guidance. Compare the trip actually needed, including airports, pickup walking, booking, payment and fallback. In the passenger model, twenty paid exceptions consume the combined saving while some fare cash saving remains; valued staff time is a separate account.

Waymo: reproduce the $6,400/$6,200 fare-and-time comparison, and the $7,440 sensitivity. The fallback's twenty minutes cover the entire failed request and substitute journey's waiting, walking and coordination; only the hypothetical conventional fare is charged. Explain why a city opening or individual business profile does not establish an airport itinerary or an organization-wide payment program.

Zoox: distinguish retrofitted test SUVs, purpose-built restricted-rider operation and verified public service. Explain the conditional commercial exemption, the 2,500 introduction ceiling per twelve months and separate simultaneous-operation limits. Reproduce the 704/512 vehicle-trip throughput case, without presenting the inputs as company fleet size, fares or demand. The example does not establish a per-trip cost: replace hypothetical throughput with actual service evidence and obtain quoted operating costs before underwriting a depot. A depot plan requires actual power, property and contracting evidence.

Freight: identify current industrial, regional and highway work separately. An Aurora rear-seat support role differs from driving supervision; Kodiak industrial operation differs from its highway launch target; Waabi and PlusAI retain disclosed onboard roles. Know why a safety-case completion percentage, cumulative miles and an on-time percentage have different denominators. Price the completed delivery with acceptance, direct or transfer design, loading, release, setup and incremental recovery. Reproduce the $98,400 direct/$42,400 transfer case, the 232-load threshold and $123 headroom before replacing assumed inputs with trial evidence.

Authorization: distinguish September 2025 statutory effectiveness, implementing rules and May 28, 2026 enforcement. Apply the actual human-driver definition rather than infer a role from somebody in the cab. Match holder and vehicle records, responder plans, domain and contract. Nonexpiry leaves material-information updates due within thirty days; that clock is not a universal enforcement cure. Reproduce the $5,500 preparedness/$12,700 interruption case while excluding the unchanged base freight spend.

## Carry this method into the next story

Identify the business decision, controlling primary record, actual change, affected parties, and economic mechanism. Then work a realistic comparison and write the recommendation that follows from it. Keep the quantity, status, geography, date, and uncertainty attached to the claim. A useful new article teaches a decision that the existing library does not already resolve.
