## Six concrete weaknesses and direction

1. **Writing's discovery control feels detached from its masthead.** In the accepted desktop capture, “Find an essay” occupies its own left row between the two-column introduction and the small Featured label, adding several loosely related entry points. Give discovery a deliberate position on the introduction's reading axis (right column desktop), with a clear 44px summary hit area and the expanded controls immediately below it. On phone keep the summary close to the introduction. Preserve details semantics, query/category state, Escape return focus, clear focus and URL/history behavior.
2. **The lead story and the archive use the same 104px phone art treatment.** The accepted phone lead has a large title and six-line narrow summary next to a very small two-part authored image. The art cannot carry the role its desktop scale promises. Make the lead a distinct phone composition: full-width headline, then an adequately sized art study with full-width deck rather than a split narrow deck. Keep compact/archive entries compact; do not inflate all 85 essays into feature cards. Preserve actual artwork, eager policy and motion context.
3. **Reading paths have little editorial distinction from the essay list.** The shared ReadingPaths source renders all collections as equal two-column text blocks with the same repeated action. Use an indexed directory treatment with an explicit essay count, more deliberate title/description spacing and an integrated destination cue. Keep content as the authority: no invented icons, category taxonomy or illustration. A directory differs meaningfully from the feature artwork grid and the compact archive.
4. **Topic arrival is a long single track before the sequence begins.** Source places header, multiple intro paragraphs, optional links/download, question list, update date and only then the numbered readings. At desktop, make intro and question navigation a coherent two-column briefing beneath the full-width topic title; allow the numbered sequence to resume a full reading measure. At phone preserve the natural reading order and avoid side-by-side dense text. Native topic capture must confirm exact spacing before acceptance.
5. **Reader openings accumulate several successive modes before the manuscript.** CoreWeave phone capture shows title/deck/byline/utilities, centered artwork/credit/play, Contents, a multi-link guide and then the manuscript. Preserve all those useful functions, while tightening the connection of the artwork, credits and title and making the guide an intentional editorial abstract rather than another large preamble. This is spacing and hierarchy, not copy deletion or changing the 85 dated manuscripts. Do not impose one cover layout on the existing essay/ledger/model/survey/notebook/proof/case-study forms.
6. **The ending loses the compact relationship between title, deck and art.** The accepted full phone CoreWeave capture's Read next area has long vertical gaps around each related essay. This may partly reflect offscreen artwork readiness; root should confirm from an actual ending viewport. Give related entries a stable, compact two-axis composition whose deck remains readable beside its existing study, with title spanning the full width. Do not hide or replace loaded artwork to manufacture density. Retain related-order, reading-path next item, return link and destination semantics.
