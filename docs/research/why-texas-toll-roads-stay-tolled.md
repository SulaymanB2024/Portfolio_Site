# Why Texas Toll Roads Stay Tolled — site release note

Canonical route: `/research/financial-systems/why-texas-toll-roads-stay-tolled`

This release publishes the A02 investigation as a standalone, indexable article with:

- a same-source FY2025 comparison for CTTS and Grand Parkway;
- an absolute-dollar HCTRA cash panel rather than a false closed waterfall;
- separate NTTA Trust arithmetic;
- public-transfer and private-distribution distinctions;
- SH 130, SH 288, NTE, LBJ, and NTE 35W contract treatment;
- Texas statutory sections §284.008, §366.407, §370.174, and §228.006;
- a downloadable workbook, system comparison, claim ledger, methodology, and JSON summary.

The page intentionally does not claim a statewide Texas toll-revenue denominator. Four open evidence items remain visible in the article and claim ledger. None is replaced with an estimate.
