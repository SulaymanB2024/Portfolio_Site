## distribution — About app distribution

Public distribution supports multiple merchants and requires approval. Acquisition channel is a separate choice from app distribution type.

## app-requirements — App Store requirements, section 1.2

[Shopify](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/app-store-requirements) · Current documentation · Primary platform requirements

Controls the billing assumption for a reusable paid App Store app. Requirements can change; launch approval is assumed, not demonstrated.

## revenue-share — Revenue share for Shopify App Store developers

[Shopify](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share) · Current documentation · Primary commercial terms

2.9% processing; conditional 0%/15% platform share; cumulative threshold and associated-account treatment. Agency commission and all other operating inputs are modeled separately.

## billing-intervals — Subscription billing with the Billing API

[Shopify](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/billing/manual-pricing/subscription-billing) · Current documentation · Primary developer documentation

Establishes 30-day and annual time-based intervals. The six synchronized cohorts are a modeling simplification.

## payment-terms — Partner Program Agreement, section 3.2

[Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/partners/terms#part-a) · Updated February 27, 2026 · Primary agreement

Merchant-payment dependency and twice-monthly payout periods. One- and two-period collection lags are scenario assumptions, not quoted platform guarantees.

## Model boundary

Hypothetical company and operating choices, not client history. Six 30-day periods begin after an approved working app exists, with $40,000 cash. Commercial inputs are assumed; current Shopify fee terms are sourced. Deterministic scenarios have no probability weights. No tax, refund, nonpayment or pre-launch cost is modeled.

Models are local, deterministic and require no network or credentials. Their future inputs carry no observed-data or probability status.
