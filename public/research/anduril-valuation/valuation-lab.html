What this model does not know

It does not observe Anduril’s funded backlog, segment revenue, compensation mix, current liquidity or share-class preferences. An outside claim must be entered at its fair value, not as a percentage of the whole parent company. The projected shipyard ownership terms are not treated as a completed parent-level transaction.

The cash-timing overlay is deliberately separate from valuation. Paying an employee in shares can conserve cash without making that employee free. The model retains the economic compensation cost while allowing an illustrative noncash addback for timing; it does not calculate actual share dilution, tax deductions or financing terms.

Extra collection days create additional receivables throughout the forecast and beyond 2041. Terminal growth remains funded rather than silently assuming the added receivables disappear. Stress results are deterministic comparisons, not likelihoods or confidence intervals.
