This separate benchmark solves aggregate growth from the original $71.65m of 30-day parent holder receipts, annualized once. It uses your return, terminal-growth, supply and reference-price inputs, but not your product shocks, redirection, delay or maturity override. It always uses five growth years plus a five-year fade.

Method, limits and reproducible files

The economic claim is policy-dependent. This is not audited-company free cash flow, equity ownership, a dividend guarantee or a trading target. Customer collateral is not added as cash owned by HYPE holders. No future buyback receives a second benefit through a reduced denominator.

With the standard fade, year-six growth equals 80% of the scenario's original final-year growth plus 20% of terminal growth; subsequent weights fade to terminal growth in year ten. Operating shocks keep that original anchor fixed. Immediate maturity instead starts a stable-growth perpetuity after year five. A delay postpones the entire modeled stream and terminal value; it is not a realistic no-revenue business forecast.

The implied return is the discount rate that reconciles the selected economic stream to the reference price. It is not an estimate of your realized token return. Share links encode assumptions in the URL fragment; no model input is sent to a server.
