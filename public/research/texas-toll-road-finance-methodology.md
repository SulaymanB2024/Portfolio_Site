# Texas toll-road finance methodology

## Scope

The article compares major Texas public toll systems and material traffic-risk concessions. It does not claim to cover every Texas toll facility or every dollar of statewide toll revenue.

## Fiscal-year and normalization rule

Every figure keeps its source fiscal year or transaction date. Cross-system normalization is used only when the source, fiscal period, denominator, and category schedule are materially comparable. The publication's normalized $100 chart is limited to CTTS and Grand Parkway because both rows come from the same TxDOT FY2025 report and use operating revenue as the denominator.

## Accounting and cash treatment

The analysis separates accrual revenue from cash receipts; operating expense from cash payments; depreciation from cash outflow; interest from principal; current capital purchases from their funding sources; reserve requirements from unrestricted cash; and public transfers from private equity distributions. Unavailable data is not treated as zero.

## Core formulas

- TxDOT per-$100 category: `category amount / system operating revenue × 100`
- NTTA comparison remainder: `gross Trust revenue - net operating expense - preliminary cash debt service`
- HCTRA selected-use diagnostic: `(cash operations + principal and interest + capital purchases + county mobility transfer) / reported toll revenue × 100`

The NTTA remainder is algebraic. It is not assigned to a reserve, capital fund, profit, or unrestricted cash balance unless a primary record traces that use. The HCTRA diagnostic is not a closed toll-dollar waterfall; it demonstrates why all funding sources must remain in the cash model.

## Legal classification

The analysis separates project-cost recovery, road-level debt, system-level debt and pledges, statutory tolling power, and concession revenue rights.

Current statutory references used:

- Texas Transportation Code §284.008(c)-(d)
- Texas Transportation Code §366.037 and §366.003(9-a)
- Texas Transportation Code §370.174
- Texas Transportation Code §228.006
